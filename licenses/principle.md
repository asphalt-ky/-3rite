# Principle

## Summary

This document defines the treatment of the project’s principles, ideas, methods, and conceptual frameworks.

The underlying principles of the project are not restricted as abstract ideas or conceptual frameworks. They may be freely studied, adapted, quoted, transformed, and applied in other works, provided that the source is acknowledged.

## Intended Freedom

The principles themselves are intended to remain open for interpretation, reuse, and adaptation. They are not treated as proprietary formulas, exclusive methods, or protected expressions of ownership.

In practical terms, you may:

- quote or reference the principles in other materials;
- adapt or reformulate them for educational, cultural, or practical uses;
- build upon them in new works, discussions, or implementations;
- apply the principles in contexts beyond this repository.

## Required Attribution

When the principles are reused, cited, or adapted, the original source should be identified clearly. A reasonable attribution may include:

- the project name;
- the relevant principle or document reference;
- a source link to the repository or the specific page where the principle appears.

Example:

> Adapted from the #3rite project principles, originally published in this repository.

## No Claim of Ownership Over the Principle

This policy does not claim ownership over ideas, concepts, or general methods as such. It is intended to preserve the openness of the underlying principles while preserving the integrity of the project’s documented interpretation and source attribution.

## Relationship to Other Licensing Documents

This policy governs the conceptual and methodological core of the project. It does not alter the separate rules governing:

- repository content under CC BY-SA 4.0;
- trademarks and logos under the trademark policy.
