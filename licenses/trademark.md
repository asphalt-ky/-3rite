# Trademark

## Trademark Policy

This document governs the use of the project name, wordmark, and logo associated with #3rite.

The project name "#3rite" is currently the subject of trademark registration efforts. While formal registration may not yet be complete, the word mark is treated as a protected brand identifier for the purpose of this project and this policy.

The logo is not currently presented as a registered trademark by this document. However, because the logo contains and represents the project identifier, references to the logo must follow this trademark policy to avoid confusion about endorsement or affiliation.

Separately, the logo artwork itself is protected by copyright. Reproduction, redistribution, or modification of the logo artwork without permission is not allowed, except where applicable law permits limited use.

## Purpose of Protection

This policy exists to preserve the integrity of the project identity, prevent confusion, and prohibit misleading or unauthorized commercial use. It is intended to prevent any implication of sponsorship, endorsement, approval, or official affiliation unless expressly stated in writing.

## Permitted Use

The trademark may be used in a non-commercial, accurate, and non-deceptive manner when the use is clearly informational, educational, cultural, or scholarly and does not suggest sponsorship or endorsement.

Examples of acceptable non-commercial use include:

- references in educational materials;
- critical or scholarly discussion;
- informational commentary or cultural analysis;
- fair descriptive quotation that does not imply official endorsement.

## Prohibited Use

The following uses are not permitted without prior written permission:

- commercial use of the trademark or logo in advertising, marketing, product branding, or promotional materials;
- use that implies sponsorship, endorsement, partnership, or official affiliation;
- use in product names, service names, domain names, or social media branding in a way likely to cause confusion;
- reproduction or distribution of the logo in a modified form that obscures its meaning or suggests official approval;
- use of the trademark in commercial quotations, reuse, or redistribution for profit.

## Commercial Use Restriction

The trademark and logo may not be used for commercial purposes, including quotation, reuse, or distribution in advertising, marketing, product branding, or any other commercial context, without prior written permission.

This restriction applies even when the mark is used only as a reference, if the context creates the impression of sponsorship, endorsement, or official authorization.

## Educational, Cultural, and Informational Use

Non-commercial use for educational, cultural, scholarly, informational, or similar non-profit purposes is permitted when the use is accurate, non-deceptive, and does not imply endorsement or affiliation with the project.

Any such use must remain truthful and must not mislead the public. It must clearly distinguish the referenced project from any official, endorsed, or affiliated entity.

## Attribution and Source Notice

When the trademark or logo is referenced in a non-commercial educational or informational context, the source should be stated clearly. The use should not obscure the original meaning or create the impression that the project owner endorses the user or the user’s work.

## No Official Confirmation

Use of the project name or logo does not constitute an official statement, endorsement, certification, or affiliation unless explicitly stated in writing by the project owner.

## Relationship to Other Documents

This policy applies separately from the repository content license and the principle reuse policy. It governs trademark and brand use only.

## Contact and Permission

Any use beyond the scope described in this policy, including commercial, promotional, or high-visibility use, requires prior written permission.

Permission requests should be submitted through this repository's issue tracker or another official contact channel designated by the project owner.

This document is intended as a practical policy statement and does not replace legal advice or formal trademark registration requirements.
